The Sartorial Workflow

The 4-Step Customization Process

Every garment follows our disciplined tailoring methodology to achieve a natural, effortless fit.

01
Step 01 — Cloth Selection

01 — Select Your Fabric

Begin with a fabric that reflects your style. Explore suitable textures, colours and materials to shape the character of your garment.

  • Curated Australian wools, breathable linen blends, and ceremonial jacquards
  • Guided tactile consultation for seasonal climate, weight, and natural drape
02
Step 02 — Anatomical Drafting

02 — Personalized Measurements

Personal measurements help shape a garment that complements your proportions, comfort and preferred fit.

  • Over 30 precise individual anatomical dimensions recorded
  • Analysis of shoulder slope, torso posture, arm pitch, and stance
03
Step 03 — Craft & Assembly

03 — Design & Tailoring

Select design details and finishing preferences as your garment takes shape through the tailoring process.

  • Lapel curvature (peak, notch, or shawl), button stance, and pocket stylings
  • Internal canvas structure, horsehair chest piece, and personalized silk lining
04
Step 04 — Sartorial Balance

04 — Final Fitting & Delivery

The final fitting allows the garment to be checked and adjusted where necessary before completion.

  • Precision checks for collar hug, sleeve pitch, chest balance, and trouser break
  • Millimeter-level hand refinements before your garment is presented

Coat & Suit Fabric Details

Fabric Selection Guide

Explore the primary materials available for your custom suit, blazer, or ceremonial ensemble.

01

Wool / Wool Blends

Best suited for: Business & Formal Suits

Exceptional drape, wrinkle recovery, and year-round breathability. Delivers a crisp, authoritative silhouette for executive presence and daily formal wear.

02

Poly-Viscose Blends

Best suited for: Practical Everyday Formalwear

Resilient, low-maintenance fabric with soft handfeel and durable drape. Engineered for active professional schedules and regular work wardrobes.

03

Linen / Linen Blends

Best suited for: Summer & Lightweight Suits

Naturally cooling and open-weave construction with relaxed sartorial elegance. Ideal for daytime events, summer weddings, and tropical climates.

04

Cotton Blends

Best suited for: Casual Blazers & Relaxed Tailoring

Comfortable tactile texture with versatile structure. Perfect for smart-casual separates, bespoke dress shirts, and unstructured sports jackets.

05

Velvet

Best suited for: Tuxedos & Occasion Wear

Deep, luxurious pile with a subtle light-catching sheen. Creates an unforgettable statement for black-tie galas, evening tuxedos, and milestone banquets.

06

Jacquard / Embroidered Fabrics

Best suited for: Wedding & Designer Outfits

Intricately woven self-patterns and rich brocade textures. Specially chosen for ceremonial sherwanis, groom attire, and distinctive Indo-Western pieces.

Atelier Consultation Note: During your private visit to our Vadapalani salon, our master tailors will present physical swatch bunches detailing exact fabric compositions, GSM weight specifications, weave types, seasonal shade palettes, and bespoke lining choices suited to your garment.
